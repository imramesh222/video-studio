# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 9: How Do You Feel?

Story: Every friend has a feeling today, and Pip shows what helps. Kitty is sad when her balloon flies away: a hug helps. Ducky is angry when his blocks fall: a big breath helps. Froggy is scared of a noise in the bush: holding hands helps, and it is only Birdie. Birdie's surprise makes everyone say "wow", and at sunset she is sleepy. At night Lumi plays "who feels...?".

Each feeling keeps one colour for its word pop-up (Fredoka 700, white outline):
happy yellow `#f2b705` · sad blue `#1e88e5` · angry red `#e53935` · scared purple `#8e24aa` · surprised orange `#fb8c00` · sleepy teal `#00897b`.
New things to build: faces for each feeling (eyebrows, a sad and an angry mouth, half-closed sleepy eyes, wide surprised eyes), a hug pose, a tear, a yawn.
Kind and gentle: the scary moment is short and ends with a friend. Every feeling is shown as okay. No new characters. Lines said together are one or two words.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we talk about feelings!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Let's find out how we feel!

**Scene 2 — Happy** (Rainbow Valley) — Pip and Sunny smile wide.
- PIP: I am smiling. I feel happy!
- SUNNY: I feel happy when we ride together!
- PIP: Happy!

**Scene 3 — A happy face** (Rainbow Valley)
- PIP: Can you make a happy face? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: What a big smile!

**Scene 4 — Sad** (red garden) — Kitty's balloon drifts up and away; one tear.
- KITTY: Meow. My balloon flew away.
- KITTY: I feel sad.
- PIP: Kitty is sad.

**Scene 5 — A hug helps** (red garden) — Pip hugs Kitty; her face lifts.
- PIP: When a friend is sad, we give a hug.
- KITTY: Thank you, Pip.
- KITTY: I feel better.

**Scene 6 — Happy or sad?** (red garden) — Kitty smiles and holds a new balloon.
- PIP: Is Kitty happy or sad now? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Kitty is happy!

**Scene 7 — Angry** (yellow pond) — Ducky's block tower tumbles; he stomps one foot.
- DUCKY: Quack! My blocks fell down!
- DUCKY: I feel angry!
- PIP: Ducky is angry.

**Scene 8 — A big breath helps** (yellow pond) — Pip and Ducky breathe in (they grow a little) and out.
- PIP: When we are angry, we take a big breath.
- PIP: Breathe in. And breathe out.
- DUCKY: I feel calm now.

**Scene 9 — Breathe with us** (yellow pond)
- PIP: Can you take a big breath? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: In. And out. Well done!

**Scene 10 — Build it again** (yellow pond) — they stack the blocks together, a number over each.
- PIP: Let's build it again, together!
- DUCKY: One, two, three blocks!
- DUCKY: Now I am happy!

**Scene 11 — Scared** (green forest) — a bush rustles; Froggy steps back.
- FROGGY: Ribbit! What is that noise?
- FROGGY: I feel scared.
- PIP: I am here, Froggy. Hold my hand.

**Scene 12 — It's only Birdie** (green forest) — Birdie pops out of the bush.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! It's only me!
- FROGGY: Oh! It's Birdie!
- PIP: Now Froggy feels safe.

**Scene 13 — Who was it?** (green forest)
- PIP: Who was in the bush? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: It was Birdie!

**Scene 14 — Surprised** (blue-sky meadow) — Birdie's box opens and balloons float out.
- BIRDIE: I have a surprise for you!
- ALL: Wow!
- PIP: We feel surprised!

**Scene 15 — A surprised face** (blue-sky meadow) — wide eyes, round mouths.
- PIP: Can you make a surprised face? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: Wow!

**Scene 16 — Show me the faces** (Rainbow Valley) — everyone in a row makes each face as it is named.
- PIP: Show me the faces!
- ALL: Happy! · ALL: Sad!
- ALL: Surprised!

**Scene 17 — Sleepy** (meadow, dusk) — Birdie yawns; her eyes half close.
- BIRDIE: Tweet. I feel sleepy.
- PIP: When we are sleepy, we rest.
- SUNNY: Hop in. I will drive slowly.

**Scene 18 — How do you feel tonight?** (night: stars, moon) — Lumi floats down.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! How do you feel tonight?
- PIP: We feel happy to see you, Lumi!

**Scene 19 — Lumi's game: sad** (night) — three friends, three faces: happy Pip, sad Kitty, surprised Froggy.
- LUMI: Who feels sad? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! Kitty feels sad. A hug helps.

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: sleepy** (night) — three friends: sleepy Birdie, happy Ducky, surprised Pip.
- LUMI: Who feels sleepy? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! Birdie feels sleepy.
- PIP: Shh.

**Scene 21 — Name the feelings** (night) — each face returns as it is named.
- PIP: How can we feel?
- ALL: Happy! · ALL: Sad! · ALL: Angry!
- ALL: Scared! · ALL: Sleepy!

**Scene 22 — Feelings recap** (recap studio) — each feeling returns with what helps.
- PIP: Happy: we smile. Sad: we hug.
- PIP: Angry: we breathe. Scared: we hold hands.
- PIP: Sleepy: we rest.

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Thank you for sharing your feelings!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: All your feelings are okay.
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
