# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 3: Counting 1 to 10

Story: Lumi is having a star party tonight. On the way, Pip and Sunny pick up the friends and count things in every world: 1 bus, 2 balloons, 3 fish, 4 hops, 5 apples, 6 cookies, 7 colors, 8 blocks, 9 stars… and Lumi makes 10. The sky goes from day to dusk to night.

Each number keeps one colour on screen all episode (big rounded pop-up, Fredoka 700, white outline):
1 red · 2 orange · 3 yellow · 4 green · 5 blue · 6 purple · 7 pink · 8 teal · 9 indigo · 10 gold (`NUMC` in episode.html).
Every counted object pops up with its number as it is said.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we are going to count to ten!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! We're going to Lumi's star party!

**Scene 2 — The number song** (rainbow road) — numbers 1–10 pop up in a row, one per word.
- PIP: One, two, three, four, five!
- SUNNY: Six, seven, eight, nine, ten!

**Scene 3 — One bus** (Rainbow Valley) — a big red "1" over Sunny.
- SUNNY: I am one big yellow bus!
- PIP: Can you hold up one finger? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: One!

**Scene 4 — Two balloons** (red garden) — Kitty holds two red balloons.
- KITTY: Meow! I have two red balloons!
- PIP: One, two! Two balloons!

**Scene 5 — Three fish** (yellow pond) — orange fish jump out one at a time.
- DUCKY: Quack! Look, the fish are jumping!
- PIP: One… two… three!
- DUCKY: Three fish!

**Scene 6 — Four hops** (green forest, stream) — Froggy hops leaf to leaf, one hop per number.
- FROGGY: Ribbit! Watch me hop on the leaves!
- PIP + FROGGY: One… two… three… four!
- FROGGY: Four hops!

**Scene 7 — Five apples** (blue-sky meadow, Birdie's tree)
- BIRDIE: Tweet! How many apples are in my tree? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: One, two, three, four, five!
- BIRDIE: Five apples!

**Scene 8 — Count the friends** (bus on the road) — each friend pops out of a window.
- PIP: Let's count our friends on the bus!
- KITTY: One! · DUCKY: Two! · FROGGY: Three! · BIRDIE: Four!
- PIP: And me, five!

**Scene 9 — How many balls?** (recap studio, counting game) — three balls in a row.
- PIP: How many balls can you see? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: One, two, three! Three balls!

**Scene 10 — Six cookies** (meadow picnic) — cookies appear on a plate one by one.
- KITTY: Meow! I made cookies for the party!
- PIP + KITTY: One, two, three, four, five, six!
- KITTY: Six cookies!

**Scene 11 — Seven colors** (sky rainbow, 7 bands) — each band glows as it is counted.
- PIP: Look! A rainbow has seven colors!
- ALL FRIENDS: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven!

**Scene 12 — Eight blocks** (meadow) — Froggy stacks blue blocks, one per number; the tower wobbles.
- FROGGY: Ribbit! Let's build a tall tower!
- PIP: One… two… three… four… five… six… seven… eight!
- FROGGY: Eight blocks! *(clap)*

**Scene 13 — Jump and count** (Rainbow Valley) — everyone jumps on each number.
- PIP: Can you jump with us? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!

**Scene 14 — Sunset** (meadow, dusk)
- BIRDIE: Tweet! The sun is going to sleep!
- PIP: It's time for Lumi's party!

**Scene 15 — Lumi's star party** (night: stars, moon) — Lumi floats down.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! Welcome to my star party!
- ALL: Hello, Lumi!

**Scene 16 — Nine star friends** (night) — little stars twinkle on one at a time (gentle, no flashing).
- LUMI: My star friends are coming! Let's count them!
- PIP + LUMI: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine!
- LUMI: Nine star friends!

**Scene 17 — Lumi makes ten** (night)
- PIP: Nine stars and Lumi. How many stars now? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: Ten!
- LUMI: Ten stars!

**Scene 18 — Ten fingers** (night) — Pip holds both arms up; numbers 1–10 pop up.
- PIP: Wiggle your fingers! Let's count them!
- PIP: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!
- PIP: You have ten fingers!

**Scene 19 — Find number five** (counting game: 2, 5, 8)
- PIP: Which one is number five? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! The blue five!

**Scene 20 — Find number ten** (counting game: 7, 4, 10)
- PIP: Can you find number ten? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! The gold ten!
- LUMI: Hooray!

**Scene 21 — One more time** (recap studio) — all ten numbers in a row.
- PIP: Let's count to ten one more time!
- ALL: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!

**Scene 22 — Counting recap** (recap studio) — each object returns with its number.
- PIP: One bus, two balloons, three fish, four hops!
- PIP: Five apples, six cookies, seven colors!
- PIP: Eight blocks, nine stars, and ten with Lumi!

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Thank you for counting with us!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: You counted all the way to ten!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
