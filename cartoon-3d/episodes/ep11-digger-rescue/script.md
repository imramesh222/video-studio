# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 11: The Digger Rescue (pilot)

Story: Ducky's bouncy ball rolls into the mud by the pond and gets stuck. Pip calls Sunny,
Sunny's roof light glows rainbow, and the rescue team goes to find a helper. Digby the digger
arrives, scoops the ball free on a count of three, and earns the first rescue badge. Teaches
in/out, big/small, and counting to three, plus the vehicle word "digger" (reuses `VEH` from
episode 8). Pilot for the Rainbow Rescue Team series (see `rescue-missions-plan.md`); if it
does well, episodes 12–18 follow with the other helpers.

One new character: Digby the digger (orange, big round eyes on the cab, black tracks, a bucket
arm that scoops — see the series plan for look and voice). Everyone else is the existing cast.
World: Ducky's yellow pond, plus one new prop — a mud patch at its edge (dark, wet, a few
squelch bubbles when something sinks in). Gentle rules apply: Ducky is worried, never scared;
no flashing lights on Sunny's rescue glow; the siren is a soft two-note dip under the voices.

New things to build (see "What has to be built" in `rescue-missions-plan.md` for the series,
scoped here to just this episode):
- Digby: vehicle-character maker (Sunny's face style — eyes on the cab, mouth decal, lip sync)
  plus a bucket arm that scoops down and back up.
- Mud patch prop near the pond, with a soft squelch sound when the ball sinks and when Digby's
  bucket digs in.
- Sunny's rescue light: a slow rainbow glow on the roof (never flashing), triggered by the call.
- A soft two-note siren tune in `sound.py`, ducked under the voices like the other background
  sounds.
- The badge row overlay and Digby's badge (the first one — row is empty before this episode).
- The counting chant reuses existing cues (`n1 n2 n3`), so no new counting sound is needed.
- New sounds to list for Ramesh to listen to once built: squelch (mud), scoop (Digby's bucket
  digging), the two-note siren.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (yellow pond, day) — Ducky bounces her red ball by the water; Sunny and Pip pull up to visit.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Let's visit Ducky at the pond!
- DUCKY: Quack! Watch my bouncy ball!

**Scene 2 — The ball rolls away** (yellow pond, mud patch at the edge) — the ball bounces too far and rolls into the mud.
- DUCKY: Oh no! My ball!
- PIP: It rolled into the mud!

**Scene 3 — Stuck!** (mud patch) — Ducky tugs at it with her wing; it won't budge.
- DUCKY: It's stuck! I can't get it out!
- PIP: Don't worry, Ducky. We'll help!

**Scene 4 — The call** (mud patch) — Pip has an idea and calls to Sunny.
- PIP: Sunny, we need help!
- SUNNY: My light says... rescue time!

**Scene 5 — Rescue team, let's go!** (mud patch → rainbow road) — Sunny's roof glows a slow rainbow; everyone chants together.
- ALL: Rescue team, let's go!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Let's find a helper!

**Scene 6 — On the way** (rainbow road) — Sunny drives, Pip thinks about what kind of helper they need.
- PIP: We need someone big and strong!
- SUNNY: Someone who can dig!

**Scene 7 — Digby arrives** (yellow pond, mud patch) — Digby the digger rolls up and says hello.
- DIGBY: Hello! I'm Digby the digger!
- PIP: Digby, Ducky's ball is stuck in the mud!

**Scene 8 — Meet Digby** (mud patch) — Digby shows off his bucket arm.
- DIGBY: This is my bucket! I can scoop!
- PIP: Bucket!

**Scene 9 — The plan** (mud patch) — Pip asks the viewer to choose.
- PIP: Should Digby scoop, or push? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- DIGBY: Let's scoop!

**Scene 10 — Try 1** (mud patch) — the bucket reaches down, almost grabs the ball, and slips back.
- DIGBY: Almost... oops!
- PIP: Try again, Digby!

**Scene 11 — Everyone helps** (mud patch) — the count, said together (`chant`).
- ALL: Scoop! One, two, three!

**Scene 12 — Rescued!** (mud patch) — the ball pops free into the air; sparkles.
- DUCKY: My ball! It's out!
- PIP: Hooray! Out of the mud!

**Scene 13 — In and out** (mud patch) — the lesson, said plainly.
- PIP: The ball was in the mud. Now it's out!
- DUCKY: In... and out!

**Scene 14 — Thank you** (yellow pond) — Ducky thanks Digby.
- DUCKY: Thank you, Digby!
- DIGBY: You're welcome, Ducky!

**Scene 15 — What we learned** (yellow pond) — the kindness lesson.
- PIP: We helped our friend. That's teamwork!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Great job, team!

**Scene 16 — Big and small** (yellow pond) — a second quick lesson, size words.
- PIP: Digby's bucket is big!
- DUCKY: And my ball is small!

**Scene 17 — Which one is the digger?** (yellow pond, find-it game: digger, bus, car cards — reuses `VEH`)
- PIP: Which one is the digger? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! This is the digger!

**Scene 18 — Celebrate** (yellow pond) — a short dance together.
- ALL: Hooray!
- PIP: Let's dance!

**Scene 19 — The badge** (yellow pond) — Digby's rescue badge pops up and joins the row.
- PIP: Digby earns a rescue badge!
- DIGBY: My first badge!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: in or out?** (night, stars) — Lumi floats down.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! Was the ball in the mud, or out?
- LUMI: *(Hold 2 seconds)* Out! Yay!

**Scene 21 — Lumi's game: who helped?** (night)
- LUMI: Who helped today? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Digby! The digger!

**Scene 22 — Recap** (recap studio) — three short lines.
- PIP: Ducky's ball was stuck in the mud.
- PIP: Digby the digger helped us scoop it out.
- PIP: Teamwork! In and out!

**Scene 23 — Ride home** (night road) — Sunny and Digby drive side by side.
- SUNNY: Great rescue today, Digby!
- DIGBY: See you next time!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: You helped a friend today!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
