# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 10: ABC with Pip (A to H)

Story: Pip and Sunny collect letters. Each friend brings one or two: A is for apple, B is for ball, C is for cat, D is for duck, E is for egg, F is for frog, G is for grapes, H is for house. At night Lumi plays "which letter?". The first of three ABC episodes (next: I to P, then Q to Z).

Each letter keeps one colour all episode, like the numbers in episode 3 (big rounded letter, Fredoka 700, white outline):
A red · B orange · C yellow · D green · E blue · F purple · G pink · H teal (the first eight `NUMC` colours).
Letter names only: the voices say "A is for apple, B is for ball", not the letter's sound. Letter sounds were tested in Pip's voice and did not sound good.
New things to build: letter cards and letter sprites (like the number cards), an egg in a nest. The grapes come from episode 5; every other object exists.
No new characters. Lines said together are one or two words.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we learn our ABCs!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Let's find some letters!

**Scene 2 — The letter song** (rainbow road) — eight letter balloons pop up in a row, one per letter.
- PIP: A, B, C, D!
- SUNNY: E, F, G, H!

**Scene 3 — A is for apple** (blue-sky meadow, Birdie's tree) — a big red A next to an apple.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! A is for apple!
- PIP: A! Apple!

**Scene 4 — Say A** (blue-sky meadow)
- PIP: Can you say A? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + BIRDIE: A!

**Scene 5 — B is for ball** (Rainbow Valley) — a big orange B next to a bouncing ball; then the B hops over Sunny.
- PIP: B is for ball!
- SUNNY: And B is for bus! That's me!
- PIP: B! Ball!

**Scene 6 — What else starts with B?** (red garden) — Kitty holds a balloon.
- PIP: What else starts with B? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- KITTY: Balloon!

**Scene 7 — C is for cat** (red garden) — a big yellow C next to Kitty; a cookie in her paw.
- KITTY: Meow! C is for cat!
- PIP: And C is for cookie!
- KITTY: C! Cat!

**Scene 8 — Which one is C?** (red garden, find-it game: cards A, B, C)
- PIP: Which one is the letter C? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! This is C!

**Scene 9 — D is for duck** (yellow pond) — a big green D next to Ducky.
- DUCKY: Quack! D is for duck!
- PIP: D! Duck!
- DUCKY: That's me!

**Scene 10 — A, B, C, D** (yellow pond) — the four letters in a row; each bounces as it is said.
- PIP: Let's say them again!
- ALL: A! · ALL: B!
- ALL: C! · ALL: D!

**Scene 11 — E is for egg** (yellow pond) — a big blue E next to an egg in a nest.
- DUCKY: Quack! I found an egg!
- PIP: E is for egg!
- PIP: E! Egg!

**Scene 12 — Say E** (yellow pond)
- PIP: Can you say E? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + DUCKY: E!

**Scene 13 — F is for frog** (green forest) — a big purple F next to Froggy; a fish jumps, a flower nods.
- FROGGY: Ribbit! F is for frog!
- PIP: F is for fish and flower, too!
- FROGGY: F! Frog!

**Scene 14 — Who starts with F?** (green forest) — Froggy, Ducky and Kitty in a row.
- PIP: Who starts with F? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Froggy! F is for frog!

**Scene 15 — G is for grapes** (green forest) — a big pink G next to a bunch of grapes.
- FROGGY: G is for grapes!
- PIP: And G is for green grass!
- PIP: G! Grapes!

**Scene 16 — H is for house** (blue-sky meadow) — a big teal H next to the little house.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! H is for house!
- PIP: And H is for hop! Hop, hop, hop!
- BIRDIE: H! House!

**Scene 17 — Hop like Pip** (blue-sky meadow)
- PIP: Can you hop like me? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: Hop!

**Scene 18 — E, F, G, H** (Rainbow Valley) — the four letters in a row; each bounces as it is said.
- PIP: And the next four!
- ALL: E! · ALL: F!
- ALL: G! · ALL: H!

**Scene 19 — Lumi loves letters** (night: stars, moon) — Lumi floats down; the eight letters glow in the sky.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! I love letters!
- PIP: Lumi, play the letter game with us!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: B** (night, find-it game: cards D, B, G)
- LUMI: Which one is the letter B? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! B is for ball!

**Scene 21 — Lumi's game: H** (night, find-it game: cards H, A, F)
- LUMI: Which one is the letter H? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! H is for house!
- PIP: Hooray!

**Scene 22 — ABC recap** (recap studio) — each letter returns with its picture.
- PIP: A, apple. B, ball. C, cat. D, duck.
- PIP: E, egg. F, frog. G, grapes. H, house.
- PIP: Eight letters!

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Next time, we find more letters!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: You know your first letters!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
