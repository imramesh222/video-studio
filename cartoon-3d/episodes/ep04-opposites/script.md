# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 4: Opposites

Story: Pip and Sunny play the opposite game all day. Every friend they pick up shows one pair: big and small, up and down, fast and slow, in and out, loud and quiet, open and closed, happy and sad. When the sun goes down they find the last pair, day and night, and Lumi plays a guessing game.

Each pair pops up side by side (big rounded words, Fredoka 700, white outline): the first word blue `#1e88e5`, its opposite red `#e53935`.
The thing on screen always changes with the word as it is said (the balloon is up on "up", down on "down").
No new characters. Lines said together are one or two words (`chant`-style), so the voices stay in step.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we are playing the opposite game!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Let's go!

**Scene 2 — Big and small** (Rainbow Valley) — Pip stands next to Sunny.
- SUNNY: I am big!
- PIP: I am small!
- PIP: Big and small are opposites!

**Scene 3 — Which one is big?** (red garden) — Kitty holds a big red balloon and a small red balloon.
- KITTY: Meow! Which balloon is big? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! This balloon is big!
- KITTY: And this balloon is small!

**Scene 4 — Up and down** (red garden) — the big balloon floats up, then drifts down.
- KITTY: My balloon goes up!
- PIP: Up, up, up!
- PIP: And down it comes. Down, down, down.

**Scene 5 — Reach up, touch down** (red garden) — Pip and Kitty stretch up, then bend down.
- PIP: Can you reach up high? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + KITTY: Up!
- PIP + KITTY: Down!

**Scene 6 — Fast and slow** (rainbow road) — the road rushes past, then crawls.
- SUNNY: Now I go fast!
- PIP: Whee! Fast!
- SUNNY: And now I go slow.

**Scene 7 — Fast or slow?** (yellow pond) — Ducky waddles very slowly to the water.
- DUCKY: Quack! Look at me walk!
- PIP: Is Ducky fast or slow? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Ducky is slow!

**Scene 8 — In and out** (yellow pond) — Ducky hops into the pond, then out again.
- DUCKY: Quack! I jump in!
- PIP: Ducky is in the water!
- DUCKY: And I climb out!

**Scene 9 — In or out?** (yellow pond) — Ducky splashes in the pond.
- PIP: Is Ducky in or out? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Ducky is in!

**Scene 10 — Loud and quiet** (green forest) — Froggy on his leaf: a big ribbit, then a tiny one.
- FROGGY: RIBBIT! That was loud!
- PIP: Shh. Now be quiet.
- FROGGY: *(whisper)* Ribbit.

**Scene 11 — Can you be quiet?** (green forest) — everyone puts a finger to their lips.
- PIP: Can you whisper like Froggy? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: *(whisper)* So quiet!
- FROGGY: RIBBIT! So loud!

**Scene 12 — Open and closed** (green forest) — Froggy's present: lid on, then lid off.
- FROGGY: My box is closed.
- PIP: Let's open it!
- FROGGY: Now it is open! Cookies!

**Scene 13 — Happy and sad** (blue-sky meadow) — Birdie has no cookie; Pip gives her one.
- BIRDIE: Tweet. I have no cookie. I am sad.
- PIP: Here is a cookie for you!
- BIRDIE: Tweet tweet! Now I am happy!

**Scene 14 — Happy or sad?** (blue-sky meadow) — Birdie flaps and spins.
- PIP: Is Birdie happy or sad? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Birdie is happy!

**Scene 15 — The opposite dance** (Rainbow Valley) — everyone in a row: stretch up, bend down, run fast, tiptoe slow.
- PIP: Let's do the opposite dance!
- ALL: Up! · ALL: Down!
- ALL: Fast! · ALL: Slow!

**Scene 16 — Sunset** (meadow, dusk) — the sun sinks.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! Look at the sun!
- PIP: The day is almost over.

**Scene 17 — Day and night** (night: stars, moon) — Lumi floats down.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! Now it is night!
- PIP: Day and night are opposites!

**Scene 18 — Lumi's game: big or small** (night) — a big star and a small star.
- LUMI: Which star is small? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! This star is small!

**Scene 19 — Lumi's game: up or down** (night) — one balloon up in the sky, one down on the grass.
- LUMI: Which balloon is up? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! This balloon is up!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: open or closed** (night) — two boxes, one open, one closed.
- LUMI: Which box is open? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! This box is open!
- PIP: Hooray!

**Scene 21 — Say the opposite** (night) — Pip says a word, everyone answers with its opposite.
- PIP: Big! · ALL: Small!
- PIP: Up! · ALL: Down!
- PIP: Fast! · ALL: Slow!

**Scene 22 — Opposites recap** (recap studio) — each pair returns with its picture.
- PIP: Big and small. Up and down. Fast and slow.
- PIP: In and out. Loud and quiet.
- PIP: Open and closed. Happy and sad. Day and night!

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Thank you for playing with us!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: You know so many opposites!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
