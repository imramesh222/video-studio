# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 12: The Fire Truck Rescue

Story: Kitty climbs too high in the tree chasing a butterfly and can't get back down. Pip calls
Sunny, the rescue team sets off, and Ruby the fire truck arrives with a ladder that goes up and
down. The team counts the ladder's steps to five as it reaches the top, Ruby lowers Kitty safely,
and Ruby earns the second rescue badge (joining Digby's from episode 11). Teaches up/down,
counting to five, and the vehicle word "fire truck" (reuses `VEH` from episode 8). Second episode
of the Rainbow Rescue Team series (see `rescue-missions-plan.md`) — written ahead of plan, so
still waiting on episode 11's results before anything here gets built.

One new character: Ruby the fire truck (red, a ladder on top, a water hose — see the series plan
for look and voice). Kitty is the friend in trouble; everyone else is existing cast. World: the
red garden (Kitty's home world). New prop needed: a tall tree at its edge, climbable, with a
branch near the top where Kitty gets stuck. Gentle rules apply: Kitty is worried, not scared;
nobody falls; the ladder moves slowly and smoothly.

New things to build (beyond what episode 11 already adds to the series — the rescue light,
siren, and badge row are reused, not rebuilt):
- Ruby: vehicle-character maker (same approach as Digby) plus a ladder that extends in five
  visible steps and retracts.
- A tall tree prop near the red garden, with a branch Kitty can sit on.
- A new sound for the ladder extending (a soft mechanical click per step) — list it with its
  time for Ramesh to listen to once built.
- Ruby's badge, to sit next to Digby's in the row from episode 11.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (red garden, day) — Kitty chases a butterfly near a tall tree.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Look at Kitty go!
- KITTY: I'm catching a butterfly!

**Scene 2 — Climbing high** (red garden, tree) — Kitty climbs branch by branch, higher and higher.
- KITTY: Up, up, up I go!
- PIP: Careful, Kitty!

**Scene 3 — Stuck!** (tree, near the top) — Kitty looks down, worried.
- KITTY: Oh no... I can't get down!
- PIP: Don't worry, Kitty. We'll help!

**Scene 4 — The call** (red garden) — Pip calls to Sunny.
- PIP: Sunny, we need help!
- SUNNY: My light says... rescue time!

**Scene 5 — Rescue team, let's go!** (red garden → rainbow road) — Sunny's roof glows rainbow; everyone chants together.
- ALL: Rescue team, let's go!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Let's find a helper!

**Scene 6 — On the way** (rainbow road) — Sunny drives, Pip thinks about what they need.
- PIP: We need someone who can reach up high!
- SUNNY: Someone with a ladder!

**Scene 7 — Ruby arrives** (red garden, by the tree) — Ruby the fire truck rolls up and says hello.
- RUBY: Hello! I'm Ruby the fire truck!
- PIP: Ruby, Kitty is stuck up the tree!

**Scene 8 — Meet Ruby** (red garden) — Ruby shows her ladder.
- RUBY: This is my ladder! It goes up and down!
- PIP: Ladder!

**Scene 9 — The plan** (red garden) — Pip asks the viewer to choose.
- PIP: Should Ruby use the ladder, or the hose? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- RUBY: Let's use the ladder!

**Scene 10 — Try 1** (red garden, tree) — the ladder rises partway, not high enough yet.
- RUBY: A little more...
- PIP: You can do it, Ruby!

**Scene 11 — Everyone helps** (red garden, tree) — counting the ladder's steps together (`chant`) as it reaches the top.
- ALL: One, two, three, four, five!

**Scene 12 — Rescued!** (tree → red garden) — Kitty climbs onto the ladder; Ruby lowers her gently to the ground.
- KITTY: I'm coming down!
- PIP: Hooray! Safe and sound!

**Scene 13 — Up and down** (red garden) — the lesson, said plainly.
- PIP: Kitty went up the tree. Now she's come down!
- KITTY: Up... and down!

**Scene 14 — Thank you** (red garden) — Kitty thanks Ruby.
- KITTY: Thank you, Ruby!
- RUBY: You're welcome, Kitty!

**Scene 15 — What we learned** (red garden) — the lesson about asking for help.
- PIP: If you're ever stuck, call for help!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Great job, team!

**Scene 16 — Counting review** (red garden) — the ladder's five steps, said again.
- PIP: One, two, three, four, five!
- PIP: Five ladder steps!

**Scene 17 — Which one is the fire truck?** (red garden, find-it game: fire truck, bus, digger cards — reuses `VEH`)
- PIP: Which one is the fire truck? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! This is the fire truck!

**Scene 18 — Celebrate** (red garden) — a short dance together.
- ALL: Hooray!
- KITTY: Let's dance!

**Scene 19 — The badge** (red garden) — Ruby's rescue badge pops up and joins Digby's in the row.
- PIP: Ruby earns a rescue badge!
- RUBY: My first badge!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: up or down?** (night, stars) — Lumi floats down.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! Did Kitty go up, or down?
- LUMI: *(Hold 2 seconds)* Down! Yay!

**Scene 21 — Lumi's game: who helped?** (night)
- LUMI: Who helped today? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Ruby! The fire truck!

**Scene 22 — Recap** (recap studio) — three short lines.
- PIP: Kitty climbed too high in the tree.
- PIP: Ruby the fire truck brought her safely down.
- PIP: Teamwork! Up and down!

**Scene 23 — Ride home** (night road) — Sunny and Ruby drive side by side.
- SUNNY: Great rescue today, Ruby!
- RUBY: See you next time!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: You helped a friend today!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
