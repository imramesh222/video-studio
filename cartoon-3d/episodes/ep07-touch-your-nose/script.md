# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 7: Touch Your Nose

Story: Pip plays "copy me" all day. With each friend he finds one part of the body and what it does: eyes to see, ears to hear, a nose to smell, a mouth to eat, hands to clap, feet to walk and a tummy to pat. At night Lumi plays "where is it?".

Each body part keeps one colour for its word pop-up and for the ring that lights up around it (Fredoka 700, white outline):
eyes blue `#1e88e5` · ears purple `#8e24aa` · nose pink `#ec407a` · mouth red `#e53935` · hands orange `#fb8c00` · feet green `#43a047` · tummy yellow `#f2b705`.
New things to build: almost nothing. A glowing ring that sits on a body part (the glow ring exists), and arm poses for touching the nose, ears and tummy.
The friends differ, and the script uses it: Ducky has a beak, Birdie has wings, Sunny has big eyes.
No new characters. Lines said together are one or two words.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we learn about our body!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Let's play copy me!

**Scene 2 — Eyes** (Rainbow Valley) — rings light up around Pip's eyes, then Sunny's.
- PIP: I have two eyes.
- PIP: I see with my eyes!
- SUNNY: I have big blue eyes!

**Scene 3 — Blink** (Rainbow Valley) — Pip and Sunny blink on each "blink".
- PIP: Can you blink your eyes? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Blink, blink!

**Scene 4 — Ears** (red garden) — rings on Kitty's ears; a bird tweets far away.
- KITTY: Meow! I have two ears.
- PIP: We hear with our ears.
- KITTY: Listen! A little bird!

**Scene 5 — Touch your ears** (red garden) — Pip and Kitty touch their ears.
- PIP: Can you touch your ears? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + KITTY: Ears!

**Scene 6 — Nose** (red garden) — Pip and Kitty smell a flower.
- PIP: This is my nose.
- PIP: I smell with my nose.
- KITTY: Sniff, sniff. A flower!

**Scene 7 — Touch your nose** (red garden) — everyone touches their nose.
- PIP: Can you touch your nose? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + KITTY: Nose!

**Scene 8 — Mouth** (yellow pond) — a ring on Ducky's beak, then on Pip's mouth.
- DUCKY: Quack! This is my beak.
- PIP: I have a mouth.
- PIP: I eat and talk with my mouth!

**Scene 9 — Open wide** (yellow pond) — Pip opens his mouth, then smiles.
- PIP: Can you open your mouth wide? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Aaah! And now a big smile!

**Scene 10 — Hands** (green forest) — Froggy holds up two hands; Pip claps on each "clap".
- FROGGY: Ribbit! I have two hands.
- PIP: I clap with my hands.
- PIP: Clap, clap, clap!

**Scene 11 — Wave your hands** (green forest) — everyone waves.
- PIP: Can you wave your hands? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + FROGGY: Wave!

**Scene 12 — Feet** (green forest) — Froggy jumps; Pip stomps on each "stomp".
- FROGGY: I have two feet. I jump with my feet!
- PIP: I walk with my feet.
- PIP: Stomp, stomp, stomp!

**Scene 13 — Stomp your feet** (green forest)
- PIP: Can you stomp your feet? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + FROGGY: Stomp, stomp!

**Scene 14 — Tummy** (blue-sky meadow) — Birdie's tummy rumbles; Pip pats his on each "pat".
- BIRDIE: Tweet! My tummy is hungry.
- PIP: This is my tummy.
- PIP: Pat, pat, pat!

**Scene 15 — Wings and arms** (blue-sky meadow) — Birdie flaps her wings, Pip flaps his arms.
- BIRDIE: I have two wings!
- PIP: I have two arms!
- PIP: Can you flap your arms? *(Hold 2 seconds)*

**Scene 16 — Copy me** (Rainbow Valley) — everyone in a row touches each part as it is named.
- PIP: Let's play copy me!
- ALL: Nose! · ALL: Ears!
- ALL: Tummy! · ALL: Feet!

**Scene 17 — Sunset** (meadow, dusk) — the sun sinks.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! The sun is going down.
- PIP: I can see it with my eyes!

**Scene 18 — Lumi has a face** (night: stars, moon) — Lumi floats down; rings on her eyes and mouth.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! I have eyes and a mouth, too!
- PIP: And you shine, Lumi!

**Scene 19 — Lumi's game: ears** (night) — Pip stands still; a ring lights up on the answer.
- LUMI: Where are Pip's ears? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! Two ears!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: feet** (night) — Froggy stands still; a ring lights up on the answer.
- LUMI: Where are Froggy's feet? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! Two feet!
- PIP: Hooray!

**Scene 21 — Touch them with me** (night) — everyone touches each part as it is named.
- PIP: Touch them with me!
- ALL: Eyes! · ALL: Ears! · ALL: Nose!
- ALL: Mouth! · ALL: Hands! · ALL: Feet!

**Scene 22 — Body recap** (recap studio) — Pip in the middle; each part lights up with its word.
- PIP: Eyes to see. Ears to hear. A nose to smell.
- PIP: A mouth to eat. Hands to clap. Feet to walk.
- PIP: That is my body!

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Thank you for playing copy me!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: Now close your eyes.
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
