# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 5: The Fruit Picnic

Story: Pip's picnic basket is empty. On the drive, each friend brings one fruit: an apple, a banana, an orange, strawberries, grapes and a big watermelon. They share the picnic, and at night Lumi plays the fruit game.

Each fruit keeps its own colour for its word pop-up (Fredoka 700, white outline):
apple red `#e53935` · banana yellow `#f2b705` · orange orange `#fb8c00` · strawberry pink `#ec407a` · grapes purple `#8e24aa` · watermelon green `#43a047`.
New things to build: banana, orange, strawberry, grapes, watermelon (whole and a slice), picnic basket and blanket. The apple exists.
No new characters. Lines said together are one or two words.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we are having a fruit picnic!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Let's find some fruit!

**Scene 2 — The empty basket** (Rainbow Valley) — Pip holds up a basket with nothing in it.
- PIP: Oh no! My basket is empty.
- PIP: Let's fill it with fruit!

**Scene 3 — Apple** (blue-sky meadow, Birdie's tree) — Birdie drops an apple into the basket.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! I have an apple!
- PIP: An apple is red and round.
- PIP: Apple!

**Scene 4 — Say apple** (blue-sky meadow)
- PIP: Can you say apple? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + BIRDIE: Apple!

**Scene 5 — Banana** (red garden) — Kitty holds a banana.
- KITTY: Meow! I have a banana!
- PIP: A banana is yellow and long.
- KITTY: Banana!

**Scene 6 — Peel the banana** (red garden) — the peel folds down, one side per "peel".
- KITTY: First we peel it.
- PIP: Peel, peel, peel!
- KITTY: Yum!

**Scene 7 — Orange** (yellow pond) — Ducky rolls an orange along the grass.
- DUCKY: Quack! I have an orange!
- PIP: An orange is orange and round.
- DUCKY: Orange!

**Scene 8 — Which one is the orange?** (yellow pond, find-it game: apple, orange, banana)
- PIP: Which one is the orange? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! This is the orange!

**Scene 9 — Strawberry** (green forest) — Froggy finds strawberries under a leaf.
- FROGGY: Ribbit! I found strawberries!
- PIP: A strawberry is red and small.
- FROGGY: Strawberry!

**Scene 10 — Count the strawberries** (green forest) — three strawberries in a row, a number over each.
- PIP: How many strawberries? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: One, two, three! Three strawberries!

**Scene 11 — Grapes** (green forest) — a bunch of grapes hangs from a branch.
- FROGGY: Look up! Grapes!
- PIP: Grapes are purple and tiny.
- PIP: Grapes!

**Scene 12 — Watermelon** (blue-sky meadow) — Sunny arrives with a watermelon on his roof.
- SUNNY: Beep beep! I have a watermelon!
- PIP: A watermelon is big and green!
- SUNNY: Watermelon!

**Scene 13 — Inside the watermelon** (blue-sky meadow) — the watermelon opens into slices.
- PIP: What color is it inside? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Red! With little black seeds.

**Scene 14 — Big or small?** (blue-sky meadow) — the watermelon next to one strawberry.
- PIP: Which fruit is big? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: The watermelon is big!
- FROGGY: And the strawberry is small!

**Scene 15 — Picnic time** (blue-sky meadow) — a blanket, the full basket, everyone sits around it.
- PIP: Look! The basket is full!
- ALL: Picnic time!

**Scene 16 — Sharing** (blue-sky meadow) — the friends pass fruit to each other.
- KITTY: An apple for you, Birdie.
- BIRDIE: A banana for you, Kitty.
- PIP: Sharing is fun!

**Scene 17 — Yum, yum** (blue-sky meadow) — everyone rubs their tummy.
- PIP: Can you rub your tummy? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: Yum, yum!

**Scene 18 — Lumi comes** (meadow, dusk to night) — Lumi floats down over the blanket.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! What a yummy picnic!
- PIP: Lumi, play the fruit game with us!

**Scene 19 — Lumi's game: the banana** (night, find-it game: orange, banana, strawberry)
- LUMI: Which one is the banana? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! The yellow banana!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: the grapes** (night, find-it game: grapes, apple, watermelon)
- LUMI: Where are the grapes? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! The purple grapes!
- PIP: Hooray!

**Scene 21 — Say the fruits** (night) — each fruit bounces as it is named.
- PIP: Let's say them all!
- ALL: Apple! · ALL: Banana! · ALL: Orange!
- ALL: Strawberry! · ALL: Grapes! · ALL: Watermelon!

**Scene 22 — Fruit recap** (recap studio) — each fruit returns with its word.
- PIP: A red apple. A yellow banana. An orange orange.
- PIP: Red strawberries. Purple grapes.
- PIP: And a big green watermelon!

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Thank you for our fruit picnic!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: You know so many fruits!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
