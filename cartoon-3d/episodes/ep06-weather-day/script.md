# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 6: The Weather Day

Story: The weather keeps changing on the drive. It is sunny in the valley, cloudy in the garden, windy at the pond and rainy in the forest. When the rain stops, the sun comes back and makes a rainbow. At night the sky is clear, and Lumi plays the weather game.

Each kind of weather keeps one colour for its word pop-up (Fredoka 700, white outline):
sunny yellow `#f2b705` · cloudy grey-blue `#78909c` · windy teal `#00897b` · rainy blue `#1e88e5` · rainbow pink `#ec407a`.
New things to build: grey clouds that cover the sun, wind (leaves and streaks blowing across), rain (falling drops, puddles), an umbrella, a sun hat, a kite, Sunny's wipers, four weather cards for the games.
Thunder (asked for by Ramesh) is a sound only: a low, soft rumble while the sky turns a darker grey, and Pip says straight away that it is only a loud sound. No lightning, nothing that flashes. Its word pop-up is purple `#8e24aa`.
No new characters. Lines said together are one or two words.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we look at the weather!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Look up at the sky!

**Scene 2 — Sunny** (Rainbow Valley) — the sun shines, everything is bright.
- PIP: The sun is shining.
- PIP: It is sunny!
- SUNNY: Sunny, just like me!

**Scene 3 — A sun hat** (red garden) — Kitty puts on a sun hat.
- KITTY: Meow! The sun is warm.
- PIP: On a sunny day, we wear a hat.
- KITTY: My sun hat!

**Scene 4 — Is it sunny?** (red garden)
- PIP: Is it sunny? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: Yes! It is sunny!

**Scene 5 — Cloudy** (red garden) — grey clouds drift across and hide the sun.
- KITTY: Where did the sun go?
- PIP: The clouds are hiding the sun.
- PIP: It is cloudy!

**Scene 6 — Count the clouds** (red garden) — three clouds in a row, a number over each.
- PIP: How many clouds can you see? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: One, two, three! Three clouds!

**Scene 7 — Windy** (yellow pond) — leaves fly past, Ducky's feathers ruffle.
- DUCKY: Quack! My feathers!
- PIP: The wind is blowing.
- PIP: It is windy!

**Scene 8 — Blow like the wind** (yellow pond) — everyone puffs; the leaves swirl on "Whoosh".
- PIP: Can you blow like the wind? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: Whoosh!

**Scene 9 — The kite** (yellow pond) — Ducky's kite climbs on the wind.
- DUCKY: Quack! My kite flies in the wind!
- PIP: Up, up, up!

**Scene 10 — Rainy** (green forest) — one drop, then gentle rain.
- FROGGY: Ribbit! I feel a drop!
- PIP: Drip, drop. The rain is falling.
- PIP: It is rainy!

**Scene 11 — The umbrella** (green forest) — Pip opens an umbrella over himself and Froggy.
- PIP: The rain makes us wet.
- PIP: Open the umbrella!
- FROGGY: Now we are dry!

**Scene 12 — Puddles** (green forest) — Froggy hops in a puddle, a splash on "Splash".
- FROGGY: Ribbit! I love puddles!
- PIP: Can you jump in a puddle? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + FROGGY: Splash!

**Scene 13 — Thunder** (green forest) — the sky turns darker grey; a low rumble rolls on "Boom".
- FROGGY: Ribbit! What was that?
- PIP: That is thunder. It is only a loud sound.
- PIP: Boom! Let's get in the bus!

**Scene 14 — Wipers** (bus on the road, rain) — everyone inside, the wipers swing with "swish".
- SUNNY: Swish, swish go my wipers!
- PIP: We are warm and dry in the bus!

**Scene 15 — The rain stops** (blue-sky meadow) — the drops thin out, the clouds part.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! The rain is stopping.
- PIP: Here comes the sun again!

**Scene 16 — A rainbow** (blue-sky meadow, sky rainbow) — the rainbow grows across the sky.
- PIP: Sun and rain together make a... *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: Rainbow!

**Scene 17 — Hat or umbrella?** (blue-sky meadow) — a rainy card, with a hat and an umbrella under it.
- PIP: It is rainy. Do we take a hat or an umbrella? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: An umbrella!

**Scene 18 — A clear night** (night: stars, moon) — Lumi floats down.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! The sky is clear tonight.
- PIP: We can see all the stars!

**Scene 19 — Lumi's game: rainy** (night, find-it game: sunny, rainy and windy cards)
- LUMI: Which one is rainy? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! Drip, drop. Rainy!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: windy** (night, find-it game: cloudy, sunny and windy cards)
- LUMI: Which one is windy? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! Whoosh. Windy!
- PIP: Hooray!

**Scene 21 — What's the weather?** (night) — each card bounces as it is named.
- PIP: What's the weather?
- ALL: Sunny! · ALL: Cloudy!
- ALL: Windy! · ALL: Rainy!

**Scene 22 — Weather recap** (recap studio) — each card returns with its word.
- PIP: Sunny: the sun shines. Cloudy: the clouds hide the sun.
- PIP: Windy: the wind blows. Rainy: drip, drop. Thunder: boom!
- PIP: And after the rain, a rainbow!

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Thank you for watching the weather with us!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: Sunny or rainy, every day is a good day!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
