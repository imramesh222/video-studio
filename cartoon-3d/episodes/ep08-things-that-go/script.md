# Rainbow Rescue Bus — Episode 8: Things That Go

Story: Sunny wants to meet other things that go. On the road he meets a little red car and a traffic light, in the forest a train, on the pond Ducky's boat, and over the meadow a plane. Everything goes home at sunset, and Lumi plays the guessing game.

Each vehicle keeps one colour for its word pop-up, and is that colour itself (Fredoka 700, white outline):
bus yellow `#f2b705` · car red `#e53935` · train green `#43a047` · boat blue `#1e88e5` · plane purple `#8e24aa`.
New things to build: a toy train with three cars and a strip of track, a sailing boat, a small plane, a traffic light. The bus and the toy car exist.
The vehicles have no faces and do not talk; only Sunny does. No new characters. Lines said together are one or two words.

---

**Scene 1 — Hello, friends** (Rainbow Valley, day) — Sunny drives in, Pip waves from the window.
- PIP: Hello, friends! Today we look for things that go!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! I love to go!

**Scene 2 — Bus** (Rainbow Valley) — Sunny shows off; the friends wave from the windows.
- SUNNY: I am a bus!
- PIP: A bus carries many friends.
- PIP: Bus!

**Scene 3 — Wheels** (Rainbow Valley) — a ring lights up on each wheel as it is counted.
- PIP: How many wheels can you see? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: One, two! Round and round!

**Scene 4 — Car** (Rainbow Valley) — a little red car drives up beside Sunny.
- KITTY: Meow! A little red car!
- PIP: A car goes vroom, vroom!
- KITTY: Car!

**Scene 5 — Big or small?** (Rainbow Valley) — the bus and the car side by side.
- PIP: Which one is big? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: The bus is big!
- KITTY: And the car is small!

**Scene 6 — Stop and go** (Rainbow Valley) — a traffic light; the car stops on red and drives on green.
- PIP: Red light. Stop!
- PIP: Green light. Go!
- SUNNY: Beep beep!

**Scene 7 — Stop or go?** (Rainbow Valley) — the light is red.
- PIP: The light is red. Do we stop or go? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- ALL: Stop!

**Scene 8 — Train** (green forest) — a green train rolls in along the track.
- FROGGY: Ribbit! I hear a train!
- PIP: A train rides on tracks.
- FROGGY: Train!

**Scene 9 — Count the train cars** (green forest) — a number pops up over each car.
- PIP: How many train cars? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: One, two, three! Three train cars!

**Scene 10 — Choo choo** (green forest) — the train puffs a little cloud on each "choo".
- PIP: Can you say choo choo? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + FROGGY: Choo choo!

**Scene 11 — Boat** (yellow pond) — Ducky sits in a small blue boat.
- DUCKY: Quack! I have a boat!
- PIP: A boat floats on the water.
- DUCKY: Boat!

**Scene 12 — Road or water?** (yellow pond) — the boat bobs across the pond.
- PIP: Does a boat go on the road or on the water? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: On the water!
- DUCKY: Splish, splash!

**Scene 13 — Plane** (blue-sky meadow) — a purple plane crosses the sky.
- BIRDIE: Tweet! Look up! A plane!
- PIP: A plane flies in the sky.
- BIRDIE: Plane! Just like me!

**Scene 14 — Fly like a plane** (blue-sky meadow) — Pip holds his arms out and leans left and right.
- PIP: Can you fly like a plane? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP + BIRDIE: Zoom!

**Scene 15 — Up and down** (blue-sky meadow) — the plane climbs slowly, then glides down.
- PIP: The plane goes up.
- PIP: And the plane comes down.

**Scene 16 — Where does it go?** (recap studio) — three pictures: a road, water, the sky; the plane flies to the sky.
- PIP: Road, water or sky?
- PIP: Where does the plane go? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- PIP: In the sky!

**Scene 17 — The parade** (Rainbow Valley) — bus, car and train in a row, the boat and plane behind.
- SUNNY: Beep beep!
- KITTY: Vroom, vroom!
- FROGGY: Choo choo!

**Scene 18 — Lumi goes, too** (night: stars, moon) — Lumi glides across the sky.
- LUMI: Hello, friends! I go across the sky!
- PIP: Like a shooting star!

**Scene 19 — Lumi's game: the train** (night, find-it game: car, train, boat)
- LUMI: Which one is the train? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! The green train!

**Scene 20 — Lumi's game: the plane** (night, find-it game: plane, bus, boat)
- LUMI: Which one flies in the sky? *(Hold 2 seconds)*
- LUMI: Yes! The purple plane!
- PIP: Hooray!

**Scene 21 — What goes?** (night) — each vehicle rolls forward as it is named.
- PIP: What goes?
- ALL: Bus! · ALL: Car! · ALL: Train!
- ALL: Boat! · ALL: Plane!

**Scene 22 — Things that go recap** (recap studio) — each vehicle returns with its word.
- PIP: The bus and the car go on the road.
- PIP: The train goes on the tracks. The boat goes on the water.
- PIP: And the plane goes in the sky!

**Scene 23 — The ride home** (night road)
- PIP: Thank you for riding with us!
- SUNNY: Beep beep! Time to go home!

**Scene 24 — Goodnight, friends** (night, sky rainbow)
- LUMI: You know so many things that go!
- LUMI: Goodnight, friends! See you soon!
- ALL: Bye-bye! *(thanks card, subscribe card)*
